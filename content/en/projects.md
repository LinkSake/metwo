Over the years I've worked on several things, most of them are in spanish but here's a list of them if you wanna check them out!

## works

### books

- [***El oficio de las cosas*** **(short stories | 2026)**](https://eltelar.xyz/blog/felicidades-a-luis-angel-y-ana-gabriela-premio-literatura-joven-y-pecda-2026/): My first book, a collection of short stories about people and the objects they hold onto. Winner of the 2026 Premio Estatal de Literatura Joven; it will be published by the Secretaría de Cultura de Chihuahua.

### anthologies

- [***Quarantine Poetry: 2020 Homemade Poems*** **(2020)**](/post/2020/09/19/a-teaspoon-of-sun/): Includes my poem *Una cucharadita de sol*, translated into English, French, Italian and Indonesian. [PDF](/docs/quarantine-poetry.pdf).
- [***Amar Hasta Morir II*** **(2025)**](https://www.buscalibre.com.mx/libro-amar-hasta-morir-ii/9786287855502/p/64884933): Poetry anthology from Ediciones Converso that includes a poem of mine.
- [**4th Short Story Contest of the Lasallian Library Network (forthcoming)**](/post/2024/04/24/titan/): Includes my short story *Titán*.

### magazines

- [***Rastro*** **No. 3 (2026)**](https://rastrocuu.wixsite.com/rastro-revista/luis-angel-ortega): A short story of mine in this independent cultural magazine from Chihuahua. [Read the issue](https://archive.org/details/rastro-no.-3-2026/mode/2up) (PDF on the Internet Archive).

## awards

- [**Premio Estatal de Literatura Joven (2026)**](https://eltelar.xyz/blog/felicidades-a-luis-angel-y-ana-gabriela-premio-literatura-joven-y-pecda-2026/): Short story category "Nellie Campobello", for *El oficio de las cosas*.
- [**FOMAC, 8th edition (2025)**](https://eltelar.xyz/blog/luis-angel-fomac-8/): Municipal Fund for Artists and Creators of Chihuahua, emerging creators category, for the project that became *El oficio de las cosas*.

## active(ish)

- [**Master's degree (education | 2025 - now)**](https://uveg.edu.mx/index.php/es/info-mael): Master's in Education with a focus on Online Education at the Universidad Virtual de Guanajuato.

## archived

- [**Luna Sophia Terra (website | 2026)**](https://luna.eltelar.xyz/): Author website for the writer Luna Sophia Terra.
- [**Dagh Salón (website | 2021 - 2026)**](https://linksake.github.io/dagh/): Website for a beauty salon in Chihuahua.
- [**Tu código tiene memoria (website | 2026)**](https://linksake.github.io/git-basico/): A small interactive intro to Git, made as coursework for my master's.
- [**Tejiendo Relatos 10 (website | 2025)**](https://taller.eltelar.xyz/): Landing page for the 10th edition of El Telar's creative writing workshop.
- **Curcubita (collective | 2024)**: Art collective/studio founded along with some artist friends with the intention to have synergy with each other.
- [**luisangel.me v1 (website | 2020 - 2024)**](https://github.com/LinkSake/me): My previous webpage/blog made with Nextra.
- [**startpage (website | 2023)**](https://github.com/LinkSake/startpage): Minimal startpage based on several startpages I saw on [r/startpages](https://www.reddit.com/r/startpages/comments/12bobou/my_first_custom_start_page/).
- [**uwusuarios (podcast | 2021 - 2022)**](https://www.youtube.com/@uwusuarios/featured): My latest podcast where we didn't had a main theme but we talked about the topics that where on our mind. I really enjoyed my time doing this with Nessie, hopefully someday we can give it another shot.
- [**DimeQuien (website | 2018)**](https://linksake.github.io/DimeQuien/): A website in colaboration with [Daniel Aguilera](https://github.com/eagleera) and Wikipolitica to showcase the profile for different candidates for the local elections of that year.
- [**Nerdverse (podcast | 2015 - 2016)**](https://www.ivoox.com/podcast-nerdverse_sq_f1261615_1.html): It was my first podcast, I did it as a part of a podcasting community named Estudio Geek / XLR Studio and we (some colaborators and myself) talked about comics, videogames, movies and pop culture.
